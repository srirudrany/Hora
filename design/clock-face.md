# Clock face geometry

Units are face radii (outer rim = 5.0). Angles: nāḻigai dial is clockwise from 12 o'clock; sky rings are counter-clockwise from 12 o'clock (Meṣa 0° at top), like facing the southern sky.

| Ring | Inner–outer | Content | Moves |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brass bezel | 5.0–5.28 | torus, metal | — |
| Nāḻigai dial | 4.30–5.00 | 60 major ticks (every nāḻigai), 240 minor (every 15 vināḻigai), numerals every 5; underlay: day arc gold / night arc blue; Rāhu kālam, Yamagaṇḍam, Kuḷikai bands on the inner strip 4.30–4.60 | underlay recomputed daily; hand angle = −360° × (now − sunrise)/(nextSunrise − sunrise) |
| Nakṣatra | 3.35–4.20 | 27 cells numbered 1–27; 12 gold rāśi dividers with Devanagari rāśi names at r 4.07 | Sun and Moon markers at r 3.95, angle 90° + sidereal λ |
| Tithi | 2.45–3.25 | 30 cells (Śukla light, Kṛṣṇa dark, Pūrṇimā pale gold, Amāvāsyā black), numerals 1–15 twice, mid-cell karaṇa tick | ring rotation = λSun; current cell highlighted |
| Yoga | 1.75–2.35 | 27 cells; 17 (Vyatīpāta) and 27 (Vaidhṛti) kumkum | pointer at 90° + (λSun + λMoon) |
| Moon | 0–1.45 | lit-sphere Moon, light vector (sin e, 0, −cos e), e = elongation | phase continuous |
| Hub ring + hand | 1.52 → 4.98 | gold hand starts outside the Moon so the phase is never covered | — |

Radial Sun (gold) and Moon (silver) lines run from 1.55 to 3.8: where the Moon line crosses the tithi ring is the tithi, its half is the karaṇa.

Watch (2D) simplification: keep the nāḻigai dial, tithi ring (as a thin arc) and the Moon phase; show nakṣatra/yoga as text complications.
