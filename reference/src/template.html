How the sky is computed

Sun and Moon positions use Jean Meeus's algorithms (the Moon with its 60 main periodic terms), converted to the nirayana zodiac with the Lahiri (Citrā-pakṣa) ayanāṁśa. Sunrise is the Sun's upper limb at the horizon with standard refraction.

Checked against a published panchangam

For Chennai on 26 September 2026 the engine gives sunrise 5:58 am, Pūrṇimā until 10:18 pm, Pūrva Bhādrapadā until 11:32 am, Gaṇḍa yoga until 1:17 pm and Rāhu kālam from 8:59 am, matching printed Tamil panchangams to about a minute.

What the live clock shows

The current nāḻigai since today's sunrise, the five limbs in force now with their end times, and today's Rāhu kālam, Yamagaṇḍam and Kuḷikai, for the town you pick. The muhūrta filter in the film is simplified for teaching; real muhūrta selection also weighs lagna and personal stars.
